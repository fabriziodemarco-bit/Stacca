Unplug!
Privacy Policy
Effective from 18 November 2026

1In short

Unplug! does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. Everything the app needs stays on your phone.

2Who we are

Unplug! – Leave Work on Time (package com.stacca.app) is developed by an independent developer based in Italy, publishing on Google Play as deMa Media. The developer is the data controller for this app.

📧 staccalapp@gmail.com

Write to this address for any question about privacy or to exercise your rights (see point 7).

3Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information, and it does not send any data to us or to third parties.

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, camera images, photos or files.

4Data stored only on your phone

To work, the app saves on your device, and does not send to us or to anyone else:

  • your settings (end of work time, working days, alert speed, sound, vibration, voice, flash);
  • your history: for each day, the end of work time, the minutes of overtime and your streak (up to one year);
  • the status of your Premium plan, as last confirmed by Google Play.

If Android's automatic backup is turned on, this data may be included in the backup of your Google account, managed by Google. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Clear storage" in Android settings.

5Phone features used

  • Notifications, exact alarms and start after reboot — to alert you when your work time ends, even after you restart your phone.
  • Vibration — for alerts.
  • Flashlight — during "Roast me" (you can turn it off in Settings). The app only switches the light on and off: it never takes pictures or videos.
  • Voice — the voice you hear is pre-recorded audio included in the app. The app does not use the microphone and records nothing.
  • Battery optimization exemption — so that alerts arrive on time.
  • Internet — used only by Google Play to handle purchases and to show the review request.

6Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play, operated by Google LLC. Google may process data under its own Privacy Policy when:

  • you buy Premium (yearly subscription or lifetime purchase): payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. The app only asks Google Play, on your phone, whether you own Premium;
  • you rate the app from the in-app review request, which is shown and handled by Google Play;
  • the app crashes or runs slowly: Google Play may collect anonymous technical statistics (Android vitals). We only see them in aggregated form, to fix bugs.

To manage or cancel a subscription, or to ask for a refund, use your Google Play account.

7Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). Since we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access or delete, but you can always write to staccalapp@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

8Earlier versions with sign-in

Versions released before November 2026 (up to 2.6) offered an optional sign-in, managed through Supabase. That feature has been removed, and all accounts and their email addresses were permanently deleted on 18 November 2026. If you still have an old version, please update the app: nothing you saved on your phone is lost.

9Children

The app is meant for working adults and is not directed at children under 16.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting personal data, we will say so here before it happens.
